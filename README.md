ALT — AI Literacy for Teens



Think critically. Use AI responsibly. Stay in control.



ALT is an educational website designed to help teenagers understand artificial intelligence, recognize AI-generated content, evaluate online information, and develop safe, responsible AI habits.



[🌐 Live Website: ALT — AI Literacy for Teens(Click to open)](https://alt-v-bit.github.io/ALT_BOT/ALT_SAA.html)

Overview





Artificial intelligence is becoming part of everyday life, from schoolwork and online searches to social media and creative projects. ALT helps young people understand how AI works, where it can go wrong, and how to use it without sacrificing their independent thinking.



The project focuses on practical AI literacy through interactive learning experiences, real-world scenarios, and actionable advice.



Features

* AI Literacy Education: Learn how AI models work, why they can make mistakes, and what their limitations are.
* Interactive Knowledge Quiz: Test your understanding of AI through real-world scenarios.
* Myth vs. Fact: Challenge common misconceptions about artificial intelligence.
* AI Content Detection: Practice identifying text that may have been generated by AI.
* Privacy Awareness: Understand the risks of sharing personal, sensitive, or identifying information with AI tools.
* Responsible AI Usage: Learn how to use AI as a learning aid without replacing your own thinking or creativity.
* Bias and Reliability: Explore AI bias, inaccurate outputs, and the importance of independent verification.
* Practical Checklists: Follow simple steps to verify AI-generated answers and review AI-assisted schoolwork.
* ALT Bot: Explore AI literacy questions through the website's chatbot interface.



Further Reading: Discover research and educational resources from organizations such as Common Sense Media, Pew Research Center, and MIT Media Lab.



Learning Objectives



ALT encourages users to develop four core AI literacy skills:



Understand AI: Recognize how AI systems generate responses and why their outputs are not always accurate.

Question AI: Verify claims, statistics, quotations, and other information against reliable sources.

Protect Privacy: Avoid sharing sensitive personal information and understand the data policies of AI services.

Think Independently: Use AI to support learning and creativity rather than outsourcing critical thinking.

Educational Philosophy



ALT promotes a simple principle:



Use AI as a tool to strengthen your thinking, not as a substitute for it.



AI-generated information should be treated critically. Important claims should be independently verified, privacy should be protected, and users should follow their school's rules when using AI for academic work.
